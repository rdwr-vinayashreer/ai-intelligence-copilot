# 🤖 AI Intelligence Copilot

A GitHub Copilot custom agent for researching recent artificial intelligence developments and producing a concise, source-backed daily AI intelligence briefing.

The project focuses on:

- AI models
- Agentic AI
- AI engineering
- AI research
- Developer tools
- Enterprise AI
- AI security
- AI infrastructure
- Robotics and multimodal AI
- Emerging AI trends

---

# 🎯 Goal

The goal is not to create another AI news aggregator.

The goal is to build an AI intelligence agent that answers:

1. What changed in AI?
2. Why does it matter?
3. What technical ideas are behind it?
4. What trends are emerging?
5. What should an engineer learn or experiment with next?

The desired information flow is:

```text
SIGNAL
   ↓
CONTEXT
   ↓
INSIGHT
   ↓
ACTION
