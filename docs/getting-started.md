# AI Intelligence Platform

## Purpose

The AI Intelligence Platform produces source-backed intelligence briefings
based on configurable research profiles.

## Available Profiles

### general-ai

Broad AI intelligence covering:

- models
- agents
- research
- AI engineering
- developer ecosystem
- enterprise AI
- AI security
- AI infrastructure
- robotics and multimodal AI

### ai-engineering

Focused on:

- agentic AI
- AI engineering
- developer ecosystem
- AI infrastructure
- AI security
- models

## Running a Profile

The workflow accepts a `profile` input.

Example:

`general-ai`

or:

`ai-engineering`

## Execution

The platform loads:

1. Platform defaults
2. Selected profile
3. Intelligence agent rules

The intelligence agent performs research, verification,
deduplication, synthesis and briefing generation.

## Delivery

The current production delivery channel is email.

Additional delivery channels can be added without changing
the intelligence research logic.
